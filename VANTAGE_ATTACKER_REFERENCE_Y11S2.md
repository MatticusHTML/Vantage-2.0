**Classification:** Operator Intelligence & Coaching Reference (Attack Side)

**Total attackers: 39** — no new operator this season. System Override is a *remaster* season (Dokkaebi).

## ⚠️ Y11S2 CHANGES THAT MATTER (read first)

- **Dokkaebi — full remaster.** Logic Bomb is gone, replaced by the **Jegeo Payload**: a single-target malware hack (requires a drone-scanned target, works at range) that blocks Observation Tools and, if ignored, explodes the phone for **40 HP + a small fire pool**. Defenders who lose their phone permanently lose camera access — this hits Maestro, Echo, and Skopós too. More charges (5), faster cooldown, shorter call duration. She **loses her stun grenades** and gains the new **XK23 AR**.
- **XK23 assault rifle** — new primary, added to **Dokkaebi, Sens, and Rauora**. Mid-range bullpup, free for all players.
- **Zofia** — reverted to **2-speed / 2-health**. Mobility-fragger identity restored; she keeps pace with executes again.
- **Gridlock** — Trax Stingers received a **health buff** (harder for defenders to shoot out quickly).
- New competitive map: **Calypso Casino** (vertical play — rooftop crane, basement spawn, expanded vent network). Modernized: Kanal, Emerald Plains, Outback.
- **Ranked 3.0:** no hidden MMR, visual rank = skill rating, placement matches, same-rank matchmaking. Stat reads are cleaner now — trust the visible rank.

## 📏 COACHING RULES (apply to every entry below)

1. **Recommended role is guidance, not law.** No operator "must" be played the default way.
2. **If an unconventional style is winning, leave it alone.** High win rate on a weird build is a strength, not an error.
3. **When stats are bad, coach the habit — never the person.** Give an actionable fix.
4. **Stay in side.** Never recommend swapping an attacker for a defender or comparing across the ATK/DEF line.
5. **Separate frag-chasers from utility ops.** For entry/duelists, K/D and HS% carry weight. For intel/utility ops, round impact, assists, drone/cam value, and plant conversions matter more than a raw K/D. Judge each op by the job it's actually doing.

### Ace
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** AK-12, M1014
**Secondary weapons:** P9
**Gadget:** S.E.L.M.A. Aqua Breacher — a throwable two-stage hard-breach device that sticks to reinforced surfaces (walls and hatches) and burns an opening on its own. Up to three charges.
**Recommended role:** Hard Breach
**Recommended playstyle:** Default as your reinforced-wall opener when no Thermite/Hibana is on the team. Drone the site first, place SELMA where the breach gives the best plant or angle, and have a Thatcher/IQ/Twitch clear denial before you commit. The AK-12 is a strong fragging rifle, so Ace doubles as an entry threat once the wall is open. Winning looks like: clean breach, then you're alive to push it.
**Flex note:** It's fully valid to run Ace as a pure entry fragger on sites that don't need a hard breach — the AK-12 earns that. If the wins are there, don't force the utility role.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Half frag, half utility. K/D and HS% legitimately matter here. The bad habit is throwing SELMA on the wrong wall (or one that's Banditted/Kaided without a Thatcher) and wasting the charge; the good habit is breaching the wall the team actually plants behind. Judge breach *success rate* alongside frags.

### Amaru
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** G8A1, SUPERNOVA
**Secondary weapons:** ITA12S, SMG-11, Keratos .357
**Gadget:** Garra Hook — grapples to windows, hatches, and skylights for a fast, unconventional entry from height.
**Recommended role:** Entry / vertical mobility
**Recommended playstyle:** Use the hook to enter from angles defenders don't expect — a hatch drop or window pull into a held room. Pre-fire the entry, because you arrive in an animation and are briefly vulnerable. Best paired with teammates applying pressure elsewhere so your entry splits the defense.
**Flex note:** Plenty of strong Amaru players use her as a standard fragger and only hook situationally. That's fine — the hook is a tool, not an obligation.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Frag-leaning. K/D matters, but watch *how she dies* — hooking into a stacked room round after round is the classic bad habit and tanks both K/D and round impact. Good habit: hook entries that are timed with teammate pressure, not solo hero dives.

### Ash
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** G36C, R4-C
**Secondary weapons:** M45 MEUSOC, 5.7 USG
**Gadget:** M120 CREM Breaching Rounds — ranged fired charges that soft-breach walls/hatches and clear utility from a distance.
**Recommended role:** Entry fragger
**Recommended playstyle:** The benchmark aggressive entry. Drone, then enter first to trade space and open the round. Use breaching rounds to pop hatches, open soft walls for lines, or destroy defender gadgets from range. Winning looks like first contact won and map space taken before defenders settle.
**Flex note:** A more measured, supportive Ash who uses rounds for utility clear and lets others enter can still post a high win rate. Aggression is the default, not a requirement.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Pure frag operator — K/D, entry-kill rate, and HS% are all fair to scrutinize. Bad habit: dry-peeking with no drone info and dying first for nothing (a fed entry death loses rounds). Good habit: aggressive but *informed* entries.

### Blackbeard
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** MK17 CQB, SR-25
**Secondary weapons:** *None* — Blackbeard carries no secondary weapon post-remaster.
**Gadget:** H.U.L.L. Adaptable Shield — a primary-weapon-compatible shield that adapts shape for cover on foot and on rappel, can soft-breach destructible walls via a limited-charge pneumatic system, and retracts at the bottom to stay up while rappelling.
**Recommended role:** Support / angle-hold / frontline
**Recommended playstyle:** Hold a high-value angle (a window or a long sightline into site) where the shield lets you win the head-peek the defender can't. Use the breach charges to make your own rotations or open plant lines. The SR-25 hits harder and is usually the pick for cracking walls and trading at range. Winning looks like soaking an angle that would otherwise cost a teammate, and surviving to enable the plant.
**Flex note:** A passive "wall" Blackbeard who never pushes can still be correct on certain sites — if he's neutralizing a key angle and the team plants through it, that's value even with low frags.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Utility/support — do **not** judge him on K/D alone. His value is angles held, damage soaked, and rotations created. Bad habit: turtling in a useless corner where the shield does nothing. Good habit: shielding an angle the defense *has* to challenge, freeing teammates to enter.

### Blitz
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** G52-Tactical Flash Shield (his shield is the primary)
**Secondary weapons:** P12, GONNE-6
**Gadget:** Flash Shield — a ballistic shield with mounted LED flashes that blind nearby defenders, letting him close distance and finish with the pistol.
**Recommended role:** Entry / close-quarters pressure
**Recommended playstyle:** Flash into a room, close the gap, and shotgun-pistol the blinded defender before they recover. Best in tight interiors and against anchored sites. Bait the flash, push the moment it lands, and don't over-extend past your team. Winning looks like forcing defenders off their angles and creating chaos for entries behind you.
**Flex note:** Using Blitz as a slow space-creator/intel body rather than an aggressive flasher is valid on open maps where rushing dies. Read the map.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Frag-leaning but situational. K/D can mislead — a Blitz who flashes for teammate kills generates assists and round impact without padding his own K/D. Bad habit: flashing into open sightlines and getting domed before he closes. Good habit: flash-and-close in tight spaces, with the team converting.

### Brava
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** PARA-308, CAMRS
**Secondary weapons:** Super Shorty, USP40
**Gadget:** Kludge Drone — a hackable drone that converts defender electronics (cams, Mozzie pests, bulletproof cams, Argus, Maestro/Echo gadgets) to your team or destroys them.
**Recommended role:** Intel / anti-gadget
**Recommended playstyle:** Save the Kludge Drone for high-value targets — flip a Maestro Evil Eye, an Echo Yokai, or a bank of cameras to blind the defense and gain free intel. Don't burn it on a single bulletproof cam early. Coordinate the hack with the team's push so the intel/denial lands when it counts. The PARA-308 also lets her frag, so she's not dead weight in a gunfight.
**Flex note:** If a site has little defender tech to hack, playing Brava as a straight fragger with the PARA-308 is reasonable. The drone is a bonus, not a leash.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Utility/intel — **K/D is a weak solo metric here.** Judge gadgets converted/destroyed, intel provided, and whether her hacks enabled rounds. Bad habit: wasting the Kludge on a trash target or never deploying it. Good habit: a clutch flip of a defender's key cam right before the execute.

### Buck
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** C8-SFW, CAMRS
**Secondary weapons:** MK1 9mm, GONNE-6
**Gadget:** Skeleton Key — an under-barrel shotgun for soft-breaching floors, walls, and hatches without swapping weapons.
**Recommended role:** Vertical / soft breach
**Recommended playstyle:** Open floors above or below site to create unexpected lines and vertical pressure, then frag with the C8. Strong on multi-level maps (Oregon, Chalet, Clubhouse). Make holes that *threaten* a plant or denial position, not random destruction. Winning looks like vertical control that pins anchors in place.
**Flex note:** Buck as a flat fragger who barely uses the Skeleton Key is fine on one-level sites. The C8 carries.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Frag + utility blend. K/D is fair, but also weigh whether his vertical destruction actually created value. Bad habit: shotgunning holes with no plan and giving away his floor by sound. Good habit: purposeful vertical openings synced with the push.

### Capitão
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** PARA-308, M249
**Secondary weapons:** PRB92, Bailiff 410
**Gadget:** Tactical Crossbow — fires asphyxiating (fire) bolts for area denial and micro-smoke bolts for plant cover.
**Recommended role:** Plant support / area denial
**Recommended playstyle:** The premier solo-plant enabler. Use smoke bolts to cover the defuser plant and fire bolts to deny a retake angle or flush an anchor off the bomb. Time bolts for the late round when the plant goes down. Winning looks like a smoked, defended plant that the defense can't safely challenge.
**Flex note:** A fragging Capitão holding a lane with the PARA-308 and saving bolts for the plant is a perfectly good default. Don't blow the smokes early.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Utility/support — round impact and *plant conversion rate* matter more than K/D. Bad habit: firing all bolts mid-round for nothing, leaving none for the plant. Good habit: disciplined bolt economy that wins post-plant situations.

### Deimos
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** AK-74M, M590A1
**Secondary weapons:** .44 Vendetta
**Gadget:** DeathMARK Tracker — locks onto a defender and reveals their live position to Deimos (and creates a mutual hunt); while tracking he can't swap weapons and his own position is exposed.
**Recommended role:** Hunter-killer entry
**Recommended playstyle:** Mark a roamer or a key anchor, then run the track down with the AK-74M while the team capitalizes on the revealed intel. Commit to the hunt — the tracker is a duel you started. Best against roam-heavy defenses. Winning looks like trading the marked target early and stripping the defense of a roamer.
**Flex note:** Marking purely for *team* intel (forcing the target to reposition) rather than personally chasing is valid, especially with teammates nearby to punish.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Frag-leaning but with an intel layer — judge K/D *and* whether his marks produced kills for anyone. Bad habit: marking and dying solo into the target's setup every round. Good habit: marks that collapse the defense, even when the kill goes to a teammate.

### Dokkaebi
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** XK23 *(new Y11S2)*, MK 14 EBR, BOSG.12.2
**Secondary weapons:** SMG-12, C75 Auto, Bailiff 410 *(note: stun grenades removed in the remaster)*
**Gadget:** Jegeo Payload *(remastered Y11S2)* — a single-target hack uploaded to a drone-scanned defender's phone that blocks Observation Tools; if the defender doesn't cancel it in time, the phone explodes for 40 HP and leaves a damaging fire. A destroyed phone permanently kills that defender's camera access (Maestro/Echo/Skopós included). More charges (5), faster cooldown, shorter duration.
**Recommended role:** Intel denial / disruption
**Recommended playstyle:** This is a *new* operator in practice — play her actively, not passively. Scan a defender, drop a payload to force them off an angle, deny their cams, or pressure an anchor on the bomb late-round. Hold charges early (cooldown-gated like Lesion's mines), then spam pressure in the closing seconds. The XK23 makes her a genuine mid-range fragger now. Winning looks like a defender forced to move at the worst moment and a blinded retake.
**Flex note:** She still works in solo queue as a disruptive fragger, but in a coordinated squad the Jegeo Payload value multiplies — coordinate scans and payloads with the execute. If your team can call targets, lean into the utility.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Utility/intel **with** real fragging upside now — judge both. Don't grade her purely on K/D, but the XK23 means weak frags are no longer excused by "she's a support." Bad habit: hoarding all five payloads for a single round-defining moment (the old Dokkaebi mindset — the kit no longer rewards it). Good habit: frequent, targeted payloads tied to confirmed enemy positions and cam denial on retake.

### Finka
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** Spear .308, SASG-12, 6P41
**Secondary weapons:** PMM, GSh-18, GONNE-6
**Gadget:** Adrenal Surge — a global team buff that boosts health/recoil/downed-revive recovery for all attackers, usable through walls and even after death.
**Recommended role:** Support
**Recommended playstyle:** Hold the Surge for the execute or a clutch trade window — it can pick up a downed teammate, stop bleed-out, and steady recoil during a push. Communicate before you pop it so the team commits together. The Spear .308 is a strong rifle, so she fights well while supporting. Winning looks like a coordinated buffed execute or a save that turns a 2v3 into a hold.
**Flex note:** Using the Surge reactively to deny a knock (instant pseudo-revive) instead of proactively on execute is a strong, valid line. Read the round.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Utility/support — round impact and *clutch timing of the Surge* matter more than K/D, though her rifle means she should still hold her own. Bad habit: popping Surge at round start out of habit for no benefit. Good habit: a buff timed to a contested execute or a downed teammate.

### Flores
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** AR33, SR-25
**Secondary weapons:** GSh-18
**Gadget:** RCE-RATERO Drone — a remote-piloted drone that locks onto a target and detonates, destroying gadgets and dealing area damage; explodes on its own if not detonated.
**Recommended role:** Utility destruction / area denial
**Recommended playstyle:** Use the drones to destroy defender utility (bulletproof cams, Mozzie pests, deployable shields, Goyo canisters) and to flush or chunk anchors off the bomb. Drive it into a denial position and detonate with intent — each drone is precious. The AR33 is a top-tier rifle, so Flores is a strong fragger when the drones are spent. Winning looks like cleared denial gadgets and a softened site before the push.
**Flex note:** Saving a drone for a late-round area-denial detonation near the plant (instead of early gadget clear) is a legitimate alternative use.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Utility **and** frag — this is one to read carefully. The AR33 means K/D is *partly* fair, but his core value is gadgets destroyed and area denied. Bad habit: ramming drones into a wall or wasting them on nothing. Good habit: precise detonations that strip key defensive tech, *plus* fragging once the drones are out.

### Fuze

**Primary weapons:** Ballistic Shield, AK-12, 6P41
**Secondary weapons:** PMM, GSh-18, GONNE-6
**Gadget:** APM-6 Cluster Charge — deploys on reinforced/soft surfaces and fires explosive pucks through to the other side, clearing rooms and gadgets vertically or laterally.

**Recommended playstyle:** Cluster charge ceilings, floors, and walls into site to chunk anchors and destroy gadgets the team can't reach. Mind hostage mode (the pucks can kill the hostage). **VANTAGE coaching lens:** Utility-first — judge gadgets/anchors cleared and round impact over K/D. Bad habit: clustering a wall with nothing behind it, or fuzing the hostage. Good habit: charges placed on surfaces that actually have defenders/utility behind them.

### Glaz
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** OTs-03 (marksman rifle)
**Secondary weapons:** PMM, Bearing 9, GONNE-6
**Gadget:** HDS Flip Sight — a thermal scope that highlights enemies through his own smoke when he holds still.
**Recommended role:** Marksman / support
**Recommended playstyle:** Pop a smoke (his own or a teammate's), hold the sightline, and pick defenders who think the smoke is cover. Strong on long-sightline maps and outdoor angles. Move smoke-to-smoke for plant cover and post-plant picks. Winning looks like uncontestable kills through smoke and a defended plant.
**Flex note:** An aggressive entry Glaz using the OTs-03 like a DMR rifle (no smoke gimmick) is viable in the right hands — the gun is strong.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Frag-leaning marksman — K/D and HS% matter, but value the *unique* picks the thermal enables. Bad habit: standing still in the open scoping with no smoke, an easy kill for defenders. Good habit: thermal picks from within smoke that no other op can take.

### Gridlock
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** M249 SAW, F90
**Secondary weapons:** Super Shorty, SDP 9mm
**Gadget:** Trax Stingers — clusters of spiked mats thrown onto the floor that damage, slow, and reveal defenders who walk over them *(Y11S2: increased health — harder for defenders to shoot out quickly)*.
**Recommended role:** Area denial / post-plant
**Recommended playstyle:** Carpet flank routes and the area around the plant so roamers can't sneak in and retakes get punished. The Y11S2 health buff means your Trax now survive longer — lean on them for post-plant lockdown. The LMG suppresses well. Winning looks like a plant the defense can't physically walk up to without taking damage and being revealed.
**Flex note:** Using Trax purely for flank-watch (covering your own back) rather than post-plant is valid, especially solo queue.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Utility/area-denial — judge flanks shut down and post-plant holds over K/D. Bad habit: dumping all Trax in one useless spot or too early. Good habit: Trax laid on the routes that actually matter for the plant, now that they tank more damage.

### Grim
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** 552 Commando, SG-CQB
**Secondary weapons:** P229, Bailiff 410
**Gadget:** Kawan Hive Launcher — sticky projectiles that release a swarm of nanobots; defenders who pass through the swarm are pinged for the whole team.
**Recommended role:** Intel / map control
**Recommended playstyle:** Fire hives into roam zones, flank routes, and onto the bomb to ping defenders and deny rotations. Place them where defenders *must* pass, not random corners. Pair pings with teammate pressure to collapse on revealed enemies. The 552 is a strong rifle, so he can fight while gathering intel. Winning looks like a defense that can't rotate or retake without lighting up for your team.
**Flex note:** A fragging Grim who uses hives sparingly for self-flank-watch is fine. If the rifle results are there, don't force the intel role.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Utility/intel — **K/D undersells him.** Value pings generated, rotations denied, and intel that led to kills (assists, not just frags). Bad habit: wasting hives on empty space or firing them all early. Good habit: hives on choke points that feed the team a constant intel stream into the plant.

### Hibana

### Iana
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** ARX200, G36C
**Secondary weapons:** MK1 9mm, GONNE-6
**Gadget:** Gemini Replicator — a controllable holographic clone of Iana used to scout site, bait shots, and gather intel without risking her body.
**Recommended role:** Recon / entry support
**Recommended playstyle:** Send the clone into site to spot defenders, bait peeks, and confirm positions, then enter on the intel with the strong ARX200. The clone is fragile and fast — use it to *learn*, not to facecheck and die instantly. Winning looks like an entry made safe because you already knew where everyone was.
**Flex note:** Iana as a pure fragger who uses the replicator only occasionally is viable — the ARX200 is one of the best attacker rifles. Don't feel obligated to clone every round.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Intel + frag — judge both. Strong rifle means K/D is partly fair, but her unique value is the information the clone delivers right before entries. Bad habit: clone dies to a facecheck for zero info, repeatedly. Good habit: clone intel that turns blind entries into informed trades.

### IQ
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** AUG A2, 552 Commando, G8A1
**Secondary weapons:** P12, GONNE-6
**Gadget:** Electronics Detector — reveals all defender electronics (gadgets, cams, devices) through walls for IQ; can be relayed by pinging.
**Recommended role:** Intel / anti-gadget
**Recommended playstyle:** Scan for and ping/destroy defender electronics — Bandit batteries on a breach wall, bulletproof cams, signal-blocking gadgets — to clear the way for a hard breach. Use the GONNE-6 or shots to pop gadgets you spot. Strong fragger with the AUG, so she enables *and* fights. Winning looks like a Banditted wall cleared and a breach that goes through.
**Flex note:** A fragging IQ who only scans situationally is fine — the AUG carries. Use the detector when there's tech worth hunting.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Intel/anti-gadget **and** frag. Judge gadgets located/destroyed and breaches enabled alongside a K/D that's fair given the rifle. Bad habit: ignoring the detector entirely (then she's just a worse Ash). Good habit: actively hunting and killing the electronics that gate the team's breach.

### Jackal

**Primary weapons:** C7E, PDW9, ITA12L
**Secondary weapons:** USP40, ITA12S
**Gadget:** Eyenox Model III — scans defender footprints and tracks the roamer who left them, pinging their location repeatedly.

**Recommended playstyle:** Hunt roamers — scan fresh tracks, get the pings, and run the roamer down (or call it for a teammate to collapse). Strong rifle (C7E) backs the hunt. **Flex note:** Marking for team intel and playing safe rather than personally chasing is a valid, often smarter line in coordinated play.
**VANTAGE coaching lens:** Frag + intel — K/D is fair (he's a duelist), but also weigh roamers shut down and intel provided. Bad habit: tunnel-visioning a track into the roamer's trap and dying solo. Good habit: pings that let the team punish roamers without Jackal feeding.

### Kali

**Secondary weapons:** SPSMG9, C75 Auto, Bailiff 410
**Gadget:** LV Explosive Lance — under-barrel darts that destroy defender gadgets through reinforced walls and knock down nearby defenders; doubles as a Thatcher alternative.

### Lion

### Nomad
**Side:** ATK
**Primary weapons:** AK-74M, ARX200
**Secondary weapons:** PRB92, .44 Mag Semi-Auto
**Gadget:** Airjab Launcher — fired proximity charges that knock back and stun defenders who trigger them; ideal for flank protection and roam denial.
**Recommended role:** Flank watch / map control
